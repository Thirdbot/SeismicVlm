\caption{\textbf{Real captured inference on three surveys.}
Prediction-versus-ground-truth mask overlays (fault masks in red) from the
deployed joint checkpoint (\S\ref{sec:complement}), rendered end to end with no
stage stubbed: a section enters, the reader measures every quantity, and the
language model reproduces it verbatim across the seam. \textbf{Thebe} --- three
ground-truth faults at dips $75.9$--$83.5\degs$; the reader emits four objects
and narrates, e.g., the fault at $[112,76]$ at dip $69.6\degs$ and throw
$105.4$~ms, located but delineated wider than the trace (deployment Dice $0.320$
against an oracle of $0.347$, Table~\ref{tab:perdomain}). \textbf{CRACKS} --- the
reader over-emits, $24$ detections against $6$ crowdsourced strokes, the failure
those sparse labels invite. \textbf{Smeaheia} --- one interpreted fault per line;
the reader measures dip $61.4\degs$ where the stick-derived label reads
$80.7\degs$, and the seam still reports the reader's own number, never the label.
Every quantity in every chain is the vision stack's measurement; the language
states none it did not measure.}
